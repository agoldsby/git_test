# AI Agent Basics

A hands-on course, about 1–2 hours long, that runs inside your AI app. The AI is your
instructor: you'll hand it real tasks and watch it do the work on real files.

## How to start

1. **Unzip this folder** (if you haven't already).
   - Windows: right-click the zip → **Extract All** → **Extract**
   - Mac: double-click the zip
2. **Open the Claude or ChatGPT desktop app** and start a new chat that can use a folder
   on your computer. In ChatGPT, switch to **Work** mode. Then add (or choose) this
   **ai-agent-basics** folder.
3. **Type `start`** and press Enter.

That's it. Your instructor takes it from there.

**Using a browser, or can't add a folder?** Drag the original **ai-agent-basics.zip**
into any Claude or ChatGPT chat and type **start**. It works there too; you'll download
your results instead of seeing them appear in this folder.

## Good to know

- No technical skills needed. Just type in plain English.
- Everything here is fictional practice material, so it's safe to use.
- Want a fresh start? Unzip the original download again.

*(Instructions for the AI are in `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. You don't need to open them.)*
