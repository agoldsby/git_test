STYLE GUIDE: Executive Email
Use for: updates to the leadership team (CEO, VPs)

Rules:
- Subject line states the decision or headline, not the topic.
  Good: "Recommend: Guided Setup in Q3, SSO after scoping"   Bad: "Onboarding update"
- First paragraph = the bottom line (BLUF: Bottom Line Up Front), max 3 sentences.
- Then "Why" (2-3 bullets with numbers) and "Risks" (1-3 bullets).
- End with exactly what you need from them and by when.
- Under 200 words. Professional, direct, no fluff.
