FEEDBACK REQUEST: "Guided Setup" proposal — redesigning how new customers get set up in Relay
From: Priya Shah, Design Lead

Love it. This is the most important design work we'll do this year.

My proposal for the flow (wireframes in Figma):
1. Name your workspace
2. Pick your team type (marketing, engineering, ops, etc.)
3. Get a starter project pre-filled for that team type
4. Invite at least 2 teammates

Strong opinions:
- We should remove the blank project entirely for new users. Nobody has ever
  told us "I love starting from nothing."
- Step 4 should not be skippable. Collaboration is the whole point of Relay.

Worries:
- If we cut scope too far, we'll ship something that feels half-finished and
  it will hurt our brand with new users.
- I need real user interviews before we finalize. We're designing on
  assumptions right now.
