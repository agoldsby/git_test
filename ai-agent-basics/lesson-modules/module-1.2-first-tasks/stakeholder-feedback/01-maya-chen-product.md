FEEDBACK REQUEST: "Guided Setup" proposal — redesigning how new customers get set up in Relay
From: Maya Chen, Head of Product

Overall I'm strongly in favor. Onboarding is the single biggest lever we have
this quarter — if new teams don't get set up in week one, nothing else we build
matters to them.

What I like:
- Replacing the blank project. That empty screen is where we lose people.
- Making "invite teammates" part of the flow instead of hiding it in Settings.

Concerns:
- I don't want this to become a six-month redesign. I'd rather ship a simple
  v1 in Q3 and iterate than ship something perfect in Q4.
- We need a clear success metric before we build. I'd propose onboarding
  completion (65% -> 80%) as the north star, with Day-30 retention as a
  guardrail.

On SSO: I hear Sales loud and clear, but I don't think we can do both well
in Q3. I want to see the engineering estimates before I decide.
