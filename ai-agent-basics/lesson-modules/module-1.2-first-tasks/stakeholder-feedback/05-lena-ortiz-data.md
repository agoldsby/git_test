FEEDBACK REQUEST: "Guided Setup" proposal — redesigning how new customers get set up in Relay
From: Lena Ortiz, Data Analyst

Supportive. Some numbers to ground the discussion:

- Onboarding completion: 65% (target 80%). Flat for 3 quarters.
- Day-30 retention: 62%, down from 71% in January.
- Sign-ups: up 40% quarter over quarter (mostly from the new free plan).
- The biggest drop-off is between "create project" and "add 5 tasks" —
  people create a blank project and then leave.
- Teams that invite a teammate in their first 3 days retain at 2x the rate
  of teams that don't.

Caution: the retention decline is partly a mix effect. Free-plan sign-ups
retain worse than paid sign-ups. Not all of the drop is onboarding's fault,
so we should be careful about promising that Guided Setup will fix
retention on its own.

I'll build an onboarding funnel dashboard so we can measure the impact.
