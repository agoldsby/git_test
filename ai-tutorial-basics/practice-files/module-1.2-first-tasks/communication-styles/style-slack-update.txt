STYLE GUIDE: Slack Update
Use for: quick updates to the team channel (#product-team)

Rules:
- Max ~120 words. People read Slack on their phones.
- Open with one emoji + a bold one-line headline.
- 3-5 bullets, each one line.
- End with a clear ask or next step, tagging people with @name.
- Casual, friendly tone. No corporate jargon.

Example:
📊 **Onboarding numbers are in**
• Completion is flat at 65% (goal: 80%)
• Biggest drop-off: blank project → first tasks
• Teams that invite someone early retain 2x better
👉 @Priya can we review Guided Setup wireframes Tues?
